THRIVE TOOLKITS

Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what the toolkit dashboards store about you, why, where it is kept, and how to delete it. The dashboards are made by Nathan Hansen, the author of the books they go with.

What is stored

  • Your email address and password. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. Nobody, including me, can read it.
  • Your activations. Which toolkit dashboards your account has unlocked, and when. Your license key itself is not stored. Only a one-way fingerprint (hash) of it is kept, so a key can be used by one account.
  • Your dashboard entries. Everything you type or tap into a dashboard, such as your habit, your daily log, notes, and weekly design sessions.
  • Basic technical records. The hosting services keep short-lived logs, such as the time of a sign-in and an IP address, to keep the service running and secure.

Why

  • Your email and password let you sign in, confirm your account, and reset a forgotten password.
  • Your activation shows the dashboard is unlocked for you, and it lets me switch off a key that is being shared.
  • Your entries are stored so they are there when you come back and so they follow you to another phone or computer.

Your data is not sold, not shared for advertising, and not used for anything else. The site has no analytics, no tracking, and no ads.

When you enter a license key

The key is sent to Payhip, the store that sold the toolkit, to check that it is a real purchase that has not been refunded. Payhip already has your order details from your purchase. Payhip also keeps a count of how many times a key has been activated.

Where it is kept

Your account and entries are stored with Supabase, a database and sign-in service. The website itself is served by GitHub Pages. Emails, such as the confirmation and reset links, are sent through an email delivery service on my behalf. Each of these services processes the data only to provide its service.

A copy of your entries is also saved in your browser on each device you sign in on, so the dashboard works offline. Signing out removes that copy from the device.

Who can see it

Your entries are private to your account. The database only lets a signed-in person read their own entries. As the site owner, I can technically reach the database for upkeep, such as fixing a problem or switching off a shared key, but I do not read people's entries.

How to delete it

  • Delete my entries: sign in, open My account, and tap Delete my entries. This removes your entries from your account and from that device.
  • Delete my account: on the same page, tap Delete my account. This removes your account, your activations, and all of your entries.
  • If you can't sign in, contact me through the Thrive store and I will delete it for you.

Backup files you export are saved on your own device. Deleting your account does not remove those, so delete them yourself if you want them gone.

Children

The dashboards are meant for adults. Please don't create an account for a child.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact me through the Thrive store.
